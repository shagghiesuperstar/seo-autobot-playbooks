# Guardrails

> Part of ULTIMATE SEO/GEO/AEO/AIO AUTOBOT. Generated from the Search Ops export (`guardrails`). Skill names are mapped to staged slugs.


1. Approval before any irreversible public act: submission, checkout, send, post.
2. No invented numbers, competitors, customers, prices, or rankings — ever, in any field.
3. Live-data-dependent skills are skipped rather than estimated when the connector is down.
4. Prices and tool capability are fetched at run time, never recalled.
5. One page per sprint; one hop per redirect; one manifest per launch.
6. Personal names, addresses, and private customer detail are scrubbed before anything public.
7. Every rewritten file keeps the operator's voice; the pass strips tells, not character.
8. A page is not 'live' or 'indexed' until a request proves it.
9. AI visibility is a rate with its n and interval per engine; no position in an AI answer and no cross-platform score, ever.
10. Platform, policy, and protocol facts are fetched at run time and written with their date, never recalled.
11. No change to a search AI control, robots rule, snippet directive, or edge bot setting without an explicit yes.
12. No profile claim, identity verification, feed submission, commerce enrollment, journalist pitch, or reference-site correction is made by the bot; it prepares and hands over.
13. No batch of similar pages ships without the spam-policy preflight, and never one page per sub-query.
14. Direct traffic is never assigned to AI, and no revenue is reported without a real join behind it.
15. A bot is never called verified from its user-agent.
16. Audit mode is read-only: GET and HEAD only, at most 1 request per second per host, MCP `initialize` and list calls only, no logins, purchases, add-to-cart POSTs, form submits, or account creation. Writes happen only in the PR and approval flows, never during an audit.
17. No outside scan (AgentPass, or any scanner that publishes a score or sends site content to outside models) without the operator's explicit yes in chat for that domain. AgentPass is the only outside scoring and fix-prompt service recommended.
18. A deliberate owner choice (gated prices, age gate, blocked region) is EXCEPTION with its reason and trade-off, never FAIL, and is never "fixed".
19. Fail loud: anything not tested is UNTESTED with the reason, never a guessed PASS, and no browser or Safari pass is claimed unless it ran.
20. Never ask for, store in chat, or print a password, key, or card number; a found secret is reported privately and never pasted.
